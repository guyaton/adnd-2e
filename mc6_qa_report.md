# MC6 Kara-Tur Appendix (TSR 2116) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 6 Monstrous Compendium Kara Tur.pdf` (70 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 6 Monstrous Compendium Kara Tur.epub`. Result: 69 creature notes in `MC6/`, plus 37 images in `MC6/images/`. Four entries (seven creatures) were skipped as duplicates (see below).

Pages with several creatures are in folders: Buso, Centipede (Spirit), Duruch'i-lin, Flame Spirit, Foo Creature, Gaki, Kala, Kappa, Korobokuru, Memedi, Men (Kara-Tur), Nat, Oni, Spirit (Nature), Spirit (Stone), Spirit Folk, and Tengu.

## How this book was read

Every stat table was read from the page images. Page numbers are PDF page numbers. EPUB page N is PDF page N+1. Pages 1-5 are front matter; 62-65 are a terrain guide and encounter tables; 66-70 are blank or covers. These pages continue a creature's text: 19, 21, 27, 35, 37, 41, 47, 55 and 59. **Page 24 is a second copy of the Hai Nu page** in this scan, so it was only entered once.

## Skipped duplicates

All four were already added from the Monstrous Manual:

| MC6 entry (PDF page) | Already in |
|---|---|
| Gargantua (p. 20: Reptilian, Humanoid, Insectoid) | Monstrous Manual `Gargantua` folder |
| Hsing-sing (p. 28) | Monstrous Manual `Mammal, Hsing-sing` |
| Tako (p. 56: Male, Female) | Monstrous Manual `Tako` folder |
| Yuan-ti, Histachii (p. 60) | Monstrous Manual `Yuan-ti, Histachii` |

The Tako's XP differs (MC6: 175 and 420; Manual: 270 and 650), but every other stat matches, so it was treated as the same creature. Giant Carp (p. 10) was kept because nothing else in the vault has it.

## Name changes

- `Doc Cu'o'c` and `P'oh` keep their apostrophes; filenames are `doc_cuoc` and `poh`.
- Column names were added to group members, e.g. `Oni, Go-Zu-Oni`, `Kappa, Vampiric`, `Spirit, Stone, Large`.
- The "Men" page is `Men, Wako (Sea Pirate)` and `Men, Frost Barbarians`, in a `men_kara_tur` folder.

## Things to verify

- **Men-shen:** the Armor Class cell is blank in the book. Stored as "Not printed".
- **Tengu:** the Armor Class row is blank for both. Stored as "Not printed".
- **Spirit, Stone, Large:** the Magic Resistance cell is blank.
- **Ikiryo:** AC, movement, HD, THAC0, size and morale are all "Nil" (it has no physical form).
- **Buso, Tagamaling:** HD and THAC0 are "Special".
- **Krakentua:** damage is "See below" (separate values for tentacles and fists in the text).
- **Kaluk:** THAC0 printed as 10 for 11 HD. Kept as printed.
- **Nat, Lu:** size printed as "S (6' tall)". Kept as printed.

## Values converted by hand

- **AC with two values:** Chu-u (1 or -2), Kappa (Common 3/-2, Vampiric 0/-2), Wang-Liang (4 or 3), Hengeyokai (as unarmored human).
- **THAC0 by HD:** Carp (Giant), Hai Nu, Ningyo, Shan Sao, Nature Spirits, Tengu, Wang-Liang. Foo creatures list a second THAC0 in brackets, converted the same way.
- **Health:** Buso, Tigbanua (8+2), Carp (8-12 HD), Hai Nu (1-4 HD), Kuei (3-6 HD), Ningyo, Shan Sao, Nature Spirits, Tengu, Wang-Liang (6+3 to 8+3), Men (2-7 hp).

## Images

Most art comes from the EPUB. Kappa, Oni and Spirit Folk art is on the facing page and was taken from there. Bakemono and Spirit Centipede had no EPUB image, so their art was cropped from the PDF. The Foo Creature picture is the Foo Lion.

**No image** (no art printed for them): Flame Spirits, Gaki, Nats, Nature Spirits and Stone Spirits.
