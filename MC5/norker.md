Their skin is hairless, tough and leathery, anywhere from reddish brown to dark gray, and their faces are a brighter shade (males have blue or red noses). Yellow eyes and two canine fangs hanging about three inches below the upper lip; they smell of stale sweat and have foul breath. Because the hide is as good as armor, they wear none, only loincloths with trophies hung from the belt, and they favor red and blue. Tribe leaders are tougher: one ''leader'' norker (maximum 10 hit points) per 20 warriors, a sub-chief with 3 HD and AC 1 in tribes of over 100 warriors, and a chief with 4 HD and AC 0 guarded by 2d4 sub-chiefs and 3d4 leaders.'
habitat_society: A tribe holds 2d10 x 10 adult male warriors, plus females (150% as many as the warriors) and young (three times as many). The strongest rules, but only within reach of his arm. 80% of lairs are underground or deep in old ruins; the rest are surface villages taken by conquest and fenced with a walkway and gate at best. They dislike sunlight (though it does not hurt them) and are most active after dark; their villages stink because they do not grasp sanitation. They negotiate with strong parties but look for a way to double-cross them, so they are unreliable allies. Rival tribes wage constant guerrilla warfare, keeping fangs as trophies, but stop short of open war. A powerful, well-equipped hobgoblin band can command a tribe's shaky loyalty for a while, since norkers respect and fear their larger cousins.
ecology: Eat anything that moves or bleeds, though in a pinch they can live on grains or other edible plants. Lazy about getting meals, they steal rather than hunt when they can. Larger predators hunt them; the hide is tough to get through, but the flesh is edible.
