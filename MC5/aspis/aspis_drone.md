combat: Drones handle nearly all fighting, defending the nest and the larvae. They never check morale and fight to the death when defending their cow. In battle a drone rears onto its rear two legs, leaving four limbs for two weapons and two shields, improving its AC to 2. Favorite weapons are short swords and hand axes, plus a crude light-crossbow-like weapon and sometimes javelins; they cannot use bows. Immune to cold and electricity, and fire-based attacks do only half damage. When forced to retreat, drones trap the area (rockfalls, pits and other crude devices, not delicate traps), especially around the cow's chamber and the larvae rooms.
habitat_society: A nest is a set of underground chambers joined by low tunnels in soft rock or earth, with 1-3 egg chambers, up to six grub hatcheries (1d10 larvae each) and 2-4 granaries around the cow's central chamber. The chamber walls and floor are coated with thick white acid that eats wood or metal in one round and does 1d8 per round to flesh until washed off (aspis are immune). Aspis make convincing scent compounds, and tame giant ants as guardians (1d10; 10% of lairs have ten times as many).
ecology: Few natural enemies. Encounters usually come when hard times make a colony raid settlements. Cow acid is prized by alchemists for corrosive agents and for magical inks and potions.
