# MC7 Spelljammer Appendix I (TSR 2109) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 7 - Monstrous Compendium - Spelljammer Appendix I.pdf` (70 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 7 - Monstrous Compendium - Spelljammer Appendix I.epub`. Result: 53 creature notes in `MC7/`, plus 48 images in `MC7/images/`. Six entries were skipped as duplicates (see below).

Pages with several creatures are in folders: Aartuk, Plasmoid, and Wiggle (Hurwaet).

## How this book was read

The PDF has no text layer, so every stat table was read from the page images. Page numbers are PDF page numbers; EPUB page N is PDF page N+1. Pages 1-3 are the cover, credits and introduction; 66-70 are covers. These pages continue a creature's text: 5, 11, 15, 29, 43 (Plasmoid, General), 51 (Reigar shakti statistics) and 61.

## Things to verify

- **Astereater:** the Treasure cell is blank. Stored as "Not printed".
- **Lumineaux:** THAC0 and No. of Attacks are printed as dashes. Stored as "Not printed".
- **Symbiont:** THAC0 is a dash.
- **Director (Spelljammer):** several rows give one value per size (10/12/16 HD). Kept as printed with sizes labelled. The bracketed 8 HD and M size are for its "mount" form.
- **Esthetic:** HD is given in hull points (20-100), because it is a living ship.
- **Mortiss:** HD 1-100 (a colony); health is entered as 1-800.
- **Nay-Churr, Vine (Infinity), Zodar, Survivor, Ontalak:** HD are open-ended or "See below".
- **Rastipede:** morale printed as a bare "12".
- **Reigar:** the sample shakti stats on p. 51 (panther, phoenix, dragon) are not included.
