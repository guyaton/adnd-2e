# MC9 Spelljammer Appendix II (TSR 2119) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 9 - Monstrous Compendium - Spelljammer Appendix II.pdf` (66 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 9 - Monstrous Compendium - Spelljammer Appendix II.epub`. Result: 64 creature notes in `MC9/`, plus 60 images in `MC9/images/`. One duplicate was skipped (see below).

Folders: Gossamer, Grav, Grell (Colonial), Lhee, Silatic, Spirit Warrior, and Witchlight Marauders.

## How this book was read

The PDF has no text layer, so every stat table was read from the page images. Page numbers are PDF page numbers; EPUB page N is mostly PDF page N+1. Pages 1-3 are covers, credits and the index; 66 is the back cover. These pages continue a creature's text: 7, 9, 11, 17, 21, 39, 47, 55, 61 and 63.

## Skipped duplicates

Similar but different creatures were kept: Colonial Grell (vs. Grell), Star Selkie (vs. Selkie), Astro Sphinx (vs. the Manual's sphinxes), Space Owl, Space Swine, Stellar Undead.

## Name changes

- `Bloodsac (Haagathga)`, `Fal (Falmadaraatha)` and `Gonn (Gonnlingdaah)` include the full name from the text.
- The Witchlight Marauder columns are named `Witchlight Marauder, Primary` and so on.

## Things to verify

- **Constellate:** HD and XP are "N/A"; AC is "5 (-10)".
- **Dragon, Moon:** the XP row is missing from the stat block. Stored as "Not printed (variable)".
- **Gadabout, Starfly Plant:** most combat stats are N/A or Nil (they are plants).
- **Contemplator, Firebird, Silatic, Grell, Skullbird:** XP printed without commas (e.g. "13000"). Kept as printed.
- **Spirit Warrior:** HD is "Master's +10" or "+20" (they are bonded to an elven master); THAC0 and morale vary.
- **Great Dreamer:** HD 72-144; health entered as 72-1,152.
- **Witchlight Marauders:** number appearing is per previous stage (each primary hatches 2-20 secondaries; each secondary hatches 1d4 tertiaries).
- **Moon, Sun and Stellar Dragons:** the age tables are not included.
- **Xixchil:** AC is "See below" (it depends on body modifications).

## Values converted by hand

- **AC with two values:** Constellate, Gammaroid (-2/-10), Colonial Grell (philosopher and patriarch), Grav Elite, Gullion (in flight), Scro, Star Selkie (prow and human form), Stargazer.
- **Health:** Firelich (16+ HD), Noble Gossamer (58 HD), Great Dreamer, Pristatic.
