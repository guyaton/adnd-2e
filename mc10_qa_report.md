# MC10 Ravenloft Appendix (TSR 2122) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `Ravenloft Monstrous Compendium.pdf` (66 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 10 - Monstrous Compendium Ravenloft.epub`. Result: 44 creature notes in `MC10/`, plus 36 images in `MC10/images/`. Twelve creatures were skipped as duplicates (see below).

Folders: Bat (Ravenloft), Doppleganger Plant, Elemental (Ravenloft), Golem (Ravenloft), Men (Lost Ones and Madmen), and Mist Horror.

## Which file is which

The file `MC 10 Monstrous Compendium Ravenloft II.pdf` is **not** MC10. Its pages are headed "Children of the Night" and it matches the MC15 EPUB (`MC 15 Monstrous Compendium Ravenloft II.epub`), so it is MC15 (Ravenloft Appendix II). The real MC10 is `Ravenloft Monstrous Compendium.pdf`, which is what this conversion used.

## How this book was read

The PDF has no text layer. This EPUB's OCR is unusually clean, so stat blocks were taken from the EPUB text and checked against the page scans, which were read directly wherever the OCR was cut off or garbled. Page numbers are PDF page numbers; EPUB page N is PDF page N+1. Pages 1-7 are covers, credits, introduction and encounter tables; 66 is the back cover. These pages are introductions or continue a creature's text: 9, 17, 18 (Elementals, general), 24, 25 (Golems, general), 37, 39, 52, 53, 55, 57, 59 and 61.

MC10 gives some of these different XP (Greater Broken One 420 vs. 650, Doll Golem 7,000 vs. 6,000, Gargoyle Golem 16,000 vs. 14,000, Glass Golem 6,000 vs. 5,000). Everything else matches, so the Monstrous Manual values were kept.

`Scarecrow, Ravenloft` (3 HD, AC 8) was kept: it differs from MC5's Scarecrow (5 HD, AC 6). The Mechanical and Zombie golems are new and were added.

## Things to verify

- **Vampyre:** the XP value is blank in the book. Stored as "Not printed".
- **Bussengeist:** AC, HD and attacks are N/A and XP is 0 (it is a harmless-looking ghost of despair).
- **Darkling:** morale printed as a bare "9".
- **Doppleganger Plant, Podling:** every combat stat is "Varies" (it copies its victim).
- **Ravenloft Elementals:** HD, size, morale and XP are given for 8, 12 and 16 HD versions. Health is entered as 8-128 to cover all three.
- **Vampires (Dwarf, Elf, Gnome):** XP rises by 1,000 per 100 years of age.
