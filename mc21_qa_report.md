# MC21 Planescape Monstrous Compendium Appendix III stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 21 Planescape Monstrous compendium 3.pdf` (130 pages), with some illustrations from `ebook/MC 21 Planescape Monstrous compendium 3.epub`. Result: 63 creature notes in `MC21/`, plus 43 images in `MC21/images/`.

Folders: Archomental, Homunculus (Elemental), Paraelemental, Quasielemental (Negative), Quasielemental (Positive), Salamander, and Xag-ya and Xeg-yi.

**Product code:** the scan doesn't show a TSR product number, so notes cite the book as `MC21` only.

## How this book was read

The PDF is a scan with no text layer, and the EPUB's OCR is rough, so every stat table was read from the page images. Page numbers are PDF page numbers. These pages have no stat table: 1-13 (covers, introduction, ecology of the Inner Planes), 47 (elemental classification), 123-125 (appendix of non-hostile planar animals), 126-130 (index and back cover). Other pages without a table continue a creature's text or are art.

## Kept although the name is close to one already in the vault

- **Shocker, Contented One:** the new, weaker shocker type (HD 1+2); MC14's Shocker matches the Sojourner.
- **Ruvoka (Planescape):** a general entry that scales by Hit Dice; MC18 has the four named ruvoka (Brajeti, Ethilum, Kaltori, Zathosi) with fixed stats.
- **Salamander, Lesser and Salamander, Noble:** different from the Monstrous Manual `Salamander`.
- **Paraelemental (Ice, Magma, Ooze, Smoke):** not the same as MC18's Athasian `Paraelemental Beast` entries.
- **Thoqqua:** also called a rockworm, but not the same as Annual 2's `Rockworm`.

## Things to verify

- **Archomentals:** HD is given as hit points (e.g. Imix 90 hp). These are unique powers.
- **Animental:** a template; nearly every stat is "Varies".
- **Opposition (Sect) and Primal (Sect):** stats vary by class and level; bracketed values are the book's example member.
- **Paraelementals and Quasielementals:** 8, 12 or 16 HD (paraelementals) and 6, 9 or 12 HD (quasielementals), with THAC0 and XP for each size.
- **Garmorm and Xag-ya/Xeg-yi:** THAC0 and XP by Hit Dice.
- **Facet:** XP rises when several facets combine (420 for two, up to 6,000 for five).
- **Darklight:** HD and THAC0 "6+6 / 13 (but see below)": they grow by draining life.
- **Egarus, Trilloch, Homunculus:** many stats are "N/A".
- **Menglis:** morale printed as "Champion (20)". Kept as printed.
