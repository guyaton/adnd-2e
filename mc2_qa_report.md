# MC2 stat blocks: QA report

Source: MC_2_Monstrous_Compendium_text.pdf (an Internet Archive OCR scan, noticeably rougher than MC1). Result: 190 creature notes in `mc2_bestiary/`, with a folder only for pages that cover several creatures. Page numbers are PDF page numbers.

## How this book was read

The OCR text layer was parsed first, then about 45 pages with unreadable or missing cells were checked against the page images and corrected by hand (all of the hit dice that the OCR garbled, plus whole tables for Aarakocra, Ankheg, Ant, Baboon, Dolphin, Dryad, Eel, Fish, Galeb Duhr, Hawk, Ki-rin, Leech, Mold, Octopus, Rot Grub, Salamander, Sahuagin, Sandling, Shark, Sphinx, Swanmay, Wolverine, Worm, Yellow Musk Creeper & Zombie, Wereboar, Otyugh, Porcupine and Whale). Generic table headers were renamed so every name is unique, and none clash with the MC1 or MC5 sets.

## Not included

- Mammal, Minimal (PDF p. 86) and Mammal, Small (p. 87): these are one-row-per-animal reference tables of ordinary animals, very blurry in this scan, not stat blocks.
- Pages 1-13 (front matter) and 144-152 (appendix pages).

## Health field

Same rule as MC1: every die is a d8 (N HD = N to 8N, printed hit points used as printed). Left blank: Haunt, Kuo-Toa, Ankheg, Giant Ant, Leech Swarm, Brown Mold, Russet Mold, Yellow Mold, Yellow Musk Zombie. Where the book gives several creatures or ages in one cell, the health range is the HD range as printed.

## Images

149 notes have an `image` field (relative path, same scheme as MC1). There is no EPUB for this book, so each illustration was cropped from the page scan. Five crops were incomplete and were dropped (Beetle, Bulette, Hippogriff, Lamprey, Osquip). Pages with no illustration: Eel, Fish, Frog, Lurker Above, Mold, Naga, Nereid, Urchin, Worm. Creatures on the same page share one image.

## Summaries

Paraphrased summaries (`description`, `combat`, and where the book has it `habitat_society` and `ecology`) were added to the 83 creatures whose Special Attacks or Special Defenses say "See below" or "Special", plus a few that share their pages (Steeder, Trapper, Swan and similar). They are written in my own words from the OCR text, not copied.

Because this scan's OCR is rough, dice and numbers inside the book text were sometimes garbled (for example a "1d6" printed as "146"). I read those as the obvious dice, but if a number in a summary looks off, check it against the book. Groups that share one description in the book (Hawks, Guardian Daemons, Hags, Lammasu, Urchins, Kuo-toa sub-types) share the same summary text, with the type-specific details added where the book gives them.
