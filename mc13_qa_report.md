# MC13 Al-Qadim Appendix (TSR 2129) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 13 Monstrous Compendium Al-Qadim.pdf` (74 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 13 Monstrous Compendium Al-Qadim.epub`. Result: 50 creature notes in `MC13/`, plus 39 images in `MC13/images/`. Nine creatures were skipped as duplicates (see below).

Folders: Camel, Gen, Genie (Tasked), Living Idol, and Silat.

## How this book was read

This EPUB's OCR is clean, so stat blocks were taken from the EPUB text and checked against the page scans wherever the text was cut off or looked wrong (Asuras, Black Cloud of Vengeance, Camel, Gen, Noble Djinni, Architect genie, Great Ghul, Silats, Simurgh, Vishap). Page numbers are PDF page numbers; EPUB page N is PDF page N+1. Pages 1-5 are covers, credits and introduction; 62-65 are the monster list, encounter tables and a compiled statistics table; 66-74 are art and covers. These pages continue a creature's text: 19, 21, 23, 25, 26 (Tasked genies, general), 36 (Zakharan giants, general) and 43.

## Things to verify

- **Asuras:** the Armor Class cell is blank in the book. Stored as "Not printed".
- **Gen:** HD is "1/2 master's level", so THAC0, health and XP vary.
- **Living Idols:** THAC0 printed as 3 for all four, regardless of HD. Kept as printed.
- **Rom:** THAC0 "2 (5)". Kept as printed.
- **Silat:** THAC0 has a bracketed second value for each, and the adult matriarch has different AC, HD and XP.
- **Pahari:** XP printed as "650 + 400".
- **Vishap:** XP is "Variable" (it ages like a dragon); the age table is not included.

## Values converted by hand

- **AC with two values:** Hama 2 (7), Maskhi (5 or 0), Sabu Lord (males/females), Sakina (visible/invisible), Silat.
- **THAC0 by HD:** Black Cloud of Vengeance, Pahari, Silat.
- **Health:** Black Cloud (15-20 HD), Pahari (3-6 HD), Silat.
