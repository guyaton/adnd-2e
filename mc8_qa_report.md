# MC8 Outer Planes Appendix (TSR 2118) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 8 Monstrous Compendium Outer Planes.pdf` (98 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 8 Monstrous Compendium Outer Planes.epub`. Result: 77 creature notes in `MC8/`, plus 67 images in `MC8/images/`. Eleven entries were skipped as duplicates (see below).

Folders: Aasimon, Animal Lord, Archon, Baatezu, Gehreleth, Slaad, Tanar'ri, and Yugoloth. Each baatezu, tanar'ri and yugoloth has its own page in the book, so most have their own picture.

## How this book was read

The PDF has no text layer, so every stat table was read from the page images. Page numbers are PDF page numbers; EPUB page N is PDF page N+1. Pages 1-9 are front matter and the Outer Planes overview; 98 is the back cover. These pages are introductions or continue a creature's text: 10 (Aasimon), 13, 19, 21, 22 (Baatezu), 43, 45, 47, 49, 61, 63, 64 (Tanar'ri), 83 and 88 (Yugoloth).

Several of these have different XP in MC8 (e.g. Moon Dog 30,500 vs. 9,000; Phoenix 87,000 vs. 19,000; Titan 70,000 vs. 21,000), and Nightmare's THAC0 is 15 here vs. 17. The rest of each stat block matches, so they were treated as the same creature. The Monstrous Manual values were kept.

The Green, Gray and Death Slaad on p. 62 are new and were added.

## Name changes

- Em-dashes in titles are written as hyphens, e.g. `Baatezu, Greater-Amnizu`, `Tanar'ri, True-Vrock`, `Yugoloth, Lesser-Mezzoloth`.
- Celestial Lammasu is `Lammasu, Celestial`, to sit next to the existing lammasu notes.

## Things to verify

- **Solar:** HD printed as "177 hp".
- **Animal Lords:** HD printed as "122 hit points"; attacks and damage are "See below".
- **Translator:** THAC0 printed as "Miss only on a 1"; `to_hit` is stored as "Hits on 2+".
- **Hordling:** AC, movement, HD, magic resistance and size each list four options (one per hordling type). Kept as printed.
- **Nabassu:** AC "4 to -5" and HD "7+2 to 7+20" (they grow as they mature).
- **Einheriar, Warden Beast:** most stats vary by class or animal type.
- **Planetar, Solar:** THAC0 includes a weapon bonus in brackets.
- **Adamantite Dragon:** the age table is not included. Only the main stat block is.
- **Hordling attack and defense tables** (random body parts and powers) are not included.
