# MC16 Planescape Appendix (TSR 2602) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 16 Monstrous Compendium Appendix.pdf` (130 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 16 Monstrous Compendium Appendix.epub`. Result: 21 creature notes in `MC16/`, plus 20 images in `MC16/images/`.

Folders: Animal Lord, Grue, Incarnate, Mephit, and Tanar'ri.

## Most of this book was skipped

MC16 ("Being a Guide to the Multiplanar Creatures from a Prior Monstrous Compendium") is mostly a **Planescape reprint of MC8 Outer Planes**, with new art and Sigil-flavoured text. Nearly every entry was already in the vault with the same stats, so only the creatures that are new to this book were added.

**Skipped as duplicates** (matched by name to existing MC8, MC6, MC10, MC1 and Monstrous Manual notes; stats were spot-checked for the Animal Lords, Yeth Hound and mephits, not compared one by one for every reprint): all the Aasimon, Animal Lords (Cat, Hawk, Wolf), all Baatezu, Bariaur, Bebilith, Bodak, Einheriar, Foo Dog and Foo Lion, Gehreleth, Githyanki, Githzerai, Hordling, Imp and Quasit, Larva, Maelephant, Marut, Mediator, Night Hag, Nightmare, Per, Shadow Fiend, all Slaadi, all Tanar'ri except the Wastrilith, all Yugoloths, and Yeth Hound.

Some reprints give different XP from the older books (for example Yeth Hound 975 here vs. 3,000 in MC5). The existing values were kept.

The Fire, Ice, Smoke, Magma (Lava), Mist and Steam mephits were also skipped: they are the Monstrous Manual's mephits, with only small damage differences.

## What was added

- **Animal Lord, Lizard Lord** (the other three lords are in MC8).
- **Baku.**
- **Grues:** Chaggrin (earth), Harginn (fire), Ildriss (air), Varrdig (water).
- **Incarnates:** Major and Minor.
- **New mephits:** Air, Earth, Ooze, Radiant, Water, Dust, Salt, Lightning, Mineral, Ash.
- **Tanar'ri, Greater-Wastrilith.**
- **Tiefling.**
- **Vargouille.**

## How this book was read

The PDF's text layer and the EPUB OCR are both poor for this book (decorative fonts), so every stat table was read from the page images. Page numbers are PDF page numbers; EPUB page N is PDF page N+1. Pages 129-130 are the "monsters by plane" table and the back cover.

## Things to verify

- **Incarnates:** stats for minor incarnates are printed in brackets after the major ones. Alignment and diet are "See below" (each incarnate embodies a virtue or vice; the text lists them).
- **Wastrilith:** morale printed as just "Fanatic".
- **Lizard Lord:** attacks, damage and size are "See below", as for the other animal lords.

## Images

Most art comes from the EPUB. The Tiefling and Varrdig art is on a facing page and was cropped from the PDF. Dust/Salt, Earth/Ooze and Lightning/Mineral mephits share a picture with their page partner, and the two Incarnates share one picture.

**No image:** Animal Lord, Lizard Lord (the book shows no lizard lord art).
