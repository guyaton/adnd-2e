# MC14 Fiend Folio Appendix (TSR 2132) stat blocks: QA report

Source: `MC 14 Monstrous Compendium Fiend Folio.pdf` (70 pages), with illustrations from `ebook/MC 14 Monstrous Compendium Fiend Folio.epub`. Result: 39 creature notes in `MC14/`, plus 36 images in `MC14/images/`. Twenty-five creatures were skipped as duplicates (see below).

Folders: Scathe, and Sheet Ghoul and Sheet Phantom.

## Which file was used

There are two copies: `MC 14 Monstrous Compendium Fiend Folio.pdf` (70 pages) and `... - bad cover.pdf` (69 pages). The 70-page file was used.

## How this book was read

The PDF has no text layer. The EPUB's OCR is good for most pages, so stat blocks were taken from the EPUB text and checked against the page scans wherever the text was cut off or looked wrong (Achaierai, Al-mi'raj, Caterwaul, Crabman, Dune Stalker, Flawder, Retriever, Shocker, Spanner, Stwinger, Tween). Page numbers are PDF page numbers; EPUB page N is PDF page N+1. Pages 1-2 are covers and credits; 66-70 are art and covers. These pages are introductions or continue a creature's text: 16 (Gem dragons, general), 39-41 (Mephits) and 43 (Penanggalan).

## Things to verify

- **Achaierai:** HD are given as hit points (body 40 hp, each leg 15 hp), and THAC0 differs for claws (17) and beak (11).
- **Apparition:** THAC0 and damage are "See below".
- **Caterwaul:** AC "6 or better" and movement "18 or 24" (it can haste itself).
- **Penanggalan:** the body's HD is "variable"; only the detached head's 4 HD is given.
- **Terithran:** XP printed as "275" (not a standard value). Kept as printed.
- **Tween:** XP printed as "17". Kept as printed.
- **Hellcat, Ice Lizard, Khargra, Shocker, Spanner, Sussurus, Thunder Children, Xill:** XP is printed without commas.

## Values converted by hand

- **AC with two values:** Achaierai (body/legs), Dark Creeper and Dark Stalker 2 (10), Flawder 7 (-3), Gorbel 3 (10), Penanggalan (with head detached), Shocker (0 or 10), Terithran (Ethereal Plane).
- **THAC0 by HD:** Flawder, Shocker, Spanner (at 10 HD), Xill (missiles).
- **Health:** Flawder (1-3 HD), Shocker (5-10 HD), Spanner (10-15 HD), Penanggalan head.
